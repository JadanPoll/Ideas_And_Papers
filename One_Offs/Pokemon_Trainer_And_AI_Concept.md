⚔️ The Intelligence Test (The "Gym Battles") 
To level up, the Trainer and AI must enter the Evaluation Arena to test their combined intelligence. These aren't standard tests; they are benchmark battles. 

• The Creative Gym (The "Beauty Contest"): The Trainer must guide the AI to write a cohesive story with strict structural constraints. Success depends on the Trainer's nuance and the AI's creativity. 
• The Logic Gym (The "Psychic Type"): A complex logic or coding puzzle. If the AI hallucinates, it loses HP. The Trainer must use "items" (better prompt constraints) to heal the logic. 
• The Efficiency Gym (The "Speed Run"): Solving a massive data problem using the fewest tokens (energy) possible. 

🎒 The Trainer's Inventory & Mechanics 

• 🔋 Token Energy (PP - Power Points): Your AI has limited high-reasoning moves per day. Use basic moves (smaller models) for easy tasks, and save high-PP moves (reasoning models) for Gym Battles.

• 🧬 The "Nature" of the AI: When you initialize your AI, you choose or roll its personality alignment: 

	• Analytical: Boosts math/coding, slightly lower creative prose. 
	• Empathetic: Boosts conversational tone and brainstorming, lower technical execution. 
	• Chaotic: Highly creative and unpredictable, prone to critical hits (brilliant ideas) or self-inflicted confusion (hallucinations).
