In here, we will explore some interesting historical ideas. It is often quite shocking but
we went from the Wright brother's first sustained American flight to the moon landdings in less than 70 years! 66 years to be precise. Below is the relevant timeline

1903: Ohio natives Orville and Wilbur Wright fly for 12 seconds covering 120 feet
1914-1918(World War I): A decade later, wood and canvas biplanes are mass produced for combat, aviation is trekked from a hobby and absorbed intot he drive of a major global industry
1927: Charles Lindbergh completes the first solo, nonstop transatlantic flight, flying for 33.5 hours from New York to Paris
1939-1945(World War II): Aviation goes through yet another massive technological leap, transitioning from simple propelers to the invention of the first operational jet engines as well as radar technologies.
1947: Chuck Yeager breaks the sound barrier in the Bell X-1, flying faster than the speed of sound (Mach 1)
1957: The Soviet Union launching Sputnik 1, the very first artificial satellite. Amateur Radio Operators(HAM) could listen to the signals it was broadcasting overhead as it passed in cycles. The Space Race is kicked off just 54 years after the Wright brothers made ground
1961: Yuri Gagarin becomes the first human in outer space, orbiting the Earth, sending the Americans into a frenzy.
1969: Apollo 11 lands on the Moon. Neil Armstrong, also an Ohio native.
